# 🚀 About Trigya

**Trigya** is a technology-driven company focused on building modern software,
AI-powered systems, intelligent automation, and digital solutions for businesses.

We combine software engineering, artificial intelligence, automation,
and cloud technologies to create solutions that solve real-world problems.

Our focus is simple:

> **Build technology that makes businesses smarter, faster, and more efficient.**

---

## 🧠 What We Build

We work across multiple areas of modern technology.

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Building intelligent systems that can understand information,
reason over data, interact with tools, and automate business tasks.

- AI-powered applications
- LLM integrations
- AI agents
- Intelligent workflows
- Retrieval-augmented systems
- AI-assisted business processes

### ⚡ Business Automation

We help eliminate repetitive manual processes through software and automation.

- Workflow automation
- Data processing
